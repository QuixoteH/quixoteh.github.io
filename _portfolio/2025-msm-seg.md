date: 2025-03-01
---

- Conducted comprehensive comparative experiments by training and evaluating 3D segmentation models (nnU-Net, 3D-TransUNet, TransBTS, SIN2GR, UNETR++, and others) on BraTS 2024 and BraTS 2025 datasets.
- Implemented standardized evaluation protocols in the BraTS-METS benchmark, measuring Dice and HD95 for ET, TC, and WT sub-regions.
- Analyzed quantitative results to validate that MSM-Seg outperforms or matches all baselines, particularly under the category-agnostic prompting setting.
